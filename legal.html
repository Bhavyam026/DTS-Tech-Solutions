Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Dynamic Technology Solutions (DTS), based in Boisar, Maharashtra, handles information that visitors and customers choose to share through our website, enquiry channels, or service interactions.

1. Information you may provide

  • Name and contact number.
  • Email address and project or installation site location.
  • Enquiry details, requested products/services, quantities, technical requirements and communications.
  • Information needed to arrange quotations, installation, support, billing or an AMC.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive information through the website enquiry assistant.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and clarify product or service requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and coordinate site visits, installation, maintenance and technical support.
  • Manage orders, invoices, project updates, warranties and AMC-related communication.
  • Protect the website, diagnose technical problems and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Website AI assistant and third-party services

The website may offer an automated assistant to help with general product information and collect enquiry details. AI-generated information is preliminary: availability, prices, specifications, delivery dates and final commitments must be confirmed by DTS. When you send a message to the assistant, your message is sent to the DTS website backend and may be processed by the third-party AI service provider selected in the active DTS backend configuration to generate a response. The provider may change as the service configuration changes. Website hosting is provided through GitHub Pages, and the enquiry backend may be hosted through Render. These providers process technical data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Do not enter passwords, payment-card data, government identity numbers or confidential business information into the assistant.

The assistant's temporary conversation context is held in backend memory and expires after approximately 30 minutes of inactivity. This does not control or guarantee retention periods used by hosting providers or the active AI provider; those providers may have separate operational, safety and legal retention practices. DTS does not intentionally use chat content for advertising.

A WhatsApp message is not sent automatically. If you choose to open WhatsApp and manually send an enquiry, WhatsApp will process that interaction under its own terms and privacy policy. DTS does not claim that third-party services are operated or controlled by DTS.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information for third-party marketing. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with service providers supporting website hosting, enquiry handling, communications, installation or delivery; with relevant suppliers where needed to fulfil a request; or where required by law. The actual providers and processing arrangements may change as our services change.

5. Retention, security and your choices

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, service relationship, recordkeeping and applicable legal requirements. Temporary assistant context expires after approximately 30 minutes of inactivity; enquiry details that are separately retained for quotations, orders, support or legal records may be kept for longer where reasonably necessary. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the systems we operate, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, withdraw a request where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed because of verification needs, legal obligations, technical limitations or records that must be retained. Privacy requests and complaints are handled by DTS Management. You can contact DTS Management at dtsboisar@gmail.com or +91 83909 09845. DTS Management will review the request, may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity, and will respond or explain any applicable limitation.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website loads third-party resources such as fonts, icons and product images. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. We do not intentionally set analytics cookies in this static page unless an optional analytics feature is added. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

7. Children and policy updates

This website is intended for people making legitimate business or service enquiries, not for children to submit personal information. We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.

Privacy & grievance contact: DTS Management. For a privacy request or complaint, email dtsboisar@gmail.com or call +91 83909 09845.
